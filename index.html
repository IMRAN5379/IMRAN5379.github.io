About Me

Researcher working at the intersection of ocean modelling, sediment dynamics, Earth observation and computational geoscience.

I am a Physical Oceanographer and Coastal Ocean Modeler with a Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. My doctoral research focused on the numerical modelling of sediment transport and coastal processes in the Northern Bay of Bengal using the Regional Ocean Modeling System (ROMS) and the Community Sediment Transport Modeling System (CSTMS).


My broader research interests include ocean–coastal dynamics, sediment transport, hydrodynamics, wave–current interactions, Earth observation, remote sensing, hydrological modelling and computational geoscience.

Research

My research combines numerical models, satellite observations, geospatial analysis and scientific computing.

🌊 Ocean & Coastal Modelling

ROMS-based numerical modelling of coastal circulation, sediment transport, hydrodynamics and ocean–coast interactions.

🌎 Sediment Dynamics

Cohesive and non-cohesive sediment transport, riverine sediment input, seabed processes and coastal morphological evolution.

🌐 Earth Observation

Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 optical data, Google Earth Engine and satellite-based environmental monitoring.

🛰️ Geospatial Intelligence

Flood mapping, waterlogging analysis, hazard assessment, GIS and geospatial decision-support workflows.

🌧️ Hydrological Modelling

River discharge, flood processes and hydrological modelling using tools such as HEC-RAS and SWAT.

🤖 Geoscience AI

Application of machine learning and computational methods to Earth observation and environmental datasets.
